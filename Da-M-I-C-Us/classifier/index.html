CLASSIFY  ·  DEFINE POLICY  ·  COMPARE
Exploration shell: Compare is the home. The left side says what the deployment is; the right side says what the organisation permits; the circle between them is the verdict. Classify and Define Policy are how the two sides get filled.

CLASSIFY — what it is

or Classify… (guided, reference tool)
Quick entry (single values)
Factual side. Four-part strings or Use “unset” are capability classifications — no verdict attaches. Composites (+) paste in Data and Model; quick entry covers single values only. The “or Classify” link opens damicus-v2_classifier_r4.html from the same folder as this file.

DEFINE POLICY — what is permitted

Define policy… (reference tool)   
Normative side. A policy is a list of rules — matching any one is sufficient, and the most permissive matching rule decides. Rule building lives in the reference Policy Maker; paste its Copy JSON here. “Define policy” opens damicus-v2_policymaker_r9.html from the same folder as this file.
